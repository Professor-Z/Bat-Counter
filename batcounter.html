Bat Emergence Counter

Count the bats leaving your bat house at dusk. Load a video, draw a box around the house, and get back the video with a running count. Everything runs in your browser; your video is never uploaded.

1

Choose your video

A steady (tripod) shot of the house against the evening sky works best. MP4 or MOV.

Filming tips
  • Use a tripod and keep the whole house in frame with open sky around it.
  • Start recording a minute before the first bat comes out.
  • Avoid branches directly over the openings; bats behind leaves can't be seen.
  • Record at normal speed (not slow-motion), 30 or 60 fps.
2

Draw a box around your bat house

Drag a box that loosely covers the whole house. The house should turn blue and get an orange exit band around it. Bats are counted when they leave that band. Redraw any time.
